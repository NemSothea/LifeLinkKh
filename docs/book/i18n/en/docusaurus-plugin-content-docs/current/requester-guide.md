# Requester guide: asking for blood

:::tip Your family can already help
In Cambodia most blood for patients comes from relatives and friends. At the hospital's blood bank,
a relative of **any** blood type can donate, and the bank gives the patient the type they need. Ask
the blood bank how many donors they need from your family. Do this **as well as** posting in
LifeLink.
:::

## 1. Sign in

Anyone with the app can ask for blood. You don't need to register as a donor. Install it from
the [download page](https://lifelinkkh.vercel.app/en/download) and sign in with Google or Facebook.

## 2. Post a request

Tap **Request blood** on **Home** (it is also under **Me**). Fill in:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Patient blood type** | As the hospital told you |
| **Units needed** | How many units the hospital asked for |
| **Hospital** | Choose from the list |
| **Urgency** | Critical, Urgent or Routine |
| **Who should the donor ask for?** | The name a donor asks for at the hospital |
| **Callback phone number** | A Cambodian mobile number, e.g. 012 345 678. Only donors who accept see it. |

Before you send, the app reminds you that it alerts donors who can give the patient's blood
type, including **compatible** types, not only the exact same type.

## 3. Admin review

**Before any donor is alerted, an admin checks that the request is genuine.** Your request shows
**Waiting for review**, and no donor has been contacted yet.

- **Approved:** you get a notification, and LifeLink alerts matching donors near the hospital.
- **Not approved:** you see the admin's reason. If the need is still real, post a new request
  with the details corrected.

## 4. While you wait for a donor

Open your request to see:

- how many donors were alerted, and how many accepted;
- **Waiting for the first donor to accept** until someone does.

You get a notification: **"A donor accepted your request."** The donor has your number and will
call you or go to the hospital.

**No matching donor nearby yet?** Your relatives and friends can still donate at the hospital's
blood bank. Any blood type counts toward the blood your patient needs.

## 5. Close the request

- When you have enough blood, the admin marks the request fulfilled after confirming donations.
- If you no longer need blood, tap **Cancel request**. Donors already alerted can no longer accept.

## Stay safe

- **Blood is free.** By national policy, blood is free to the patient. The hospital may charge a
  fee for testing and processing; ask how much.
- **Never pay anyone for blood or a donor.** If someone offers to sell you blood, tell the hospital.
  LifeLink never asks for money.
- **Close relatives:** don't arrange for a parent's, child's or sibling's blood to go straight to
  the patient. Let them donate to the blood bank. The hospital decides how it is used.
