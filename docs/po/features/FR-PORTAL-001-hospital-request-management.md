## Problem
Hospital staff are the one party who can confirm a donation actually happened, and they have no way to
do it. They also cannot see the requests tied to their own facility, so nobody is coordinating the
donors who show up. Staff work at a desk with a browser, not on a personal phone app.

## Desired outcome
Staff sign in to a web portal, see every request for their hospital and who has accepted, and confirm
a donation once it has taken place — which is what makes the donor's history and cooldown real.

## Why
Hospital confirmation is load-bearing in two places. It is the mitigation for false and abusive
requests named in `prd.md` section 8, and it is what turns a self-reported donation into a verified
one — `prd.md` section 1 counts 50 *hospital-verified* donations, not 50 claimed ones.

Build this alongside the request flow it manages rather than at the polish milestone. `CLAUDE.md`
section 4 lists web portal polish at M6; that is polish, not first build. Registered as M4.
